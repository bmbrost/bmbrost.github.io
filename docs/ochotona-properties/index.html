This lovely duplex in the heart of Animas City is a great place to settle in and feel at home. The large eat-in kitchen has tons of counter space and storage and opens up to a bright, spacious living area perfect for cooking, hanging out, or entertaining. Big living room windows look out toward Smelter Mountain and the historic Animas City Museum, bringing in great light and views.

The home has three bedrooms and two-and-a-half bathrooms, offering plenty of space to spread out. The primary bedroom is upstairs and features its own private balcony patio with beautiful views—an ideal spot for morning coffee or winding down at the end of the day.

You’ll also enjoy a private, fenced-in backyard, central air conditioning, a large one-car garage, and attractive xeriscaping that keeps outdoor maintenance simple and water use low. The location is hard to beat—walk or bike to trailheads, coffee shops, restaurants, groceries, and the river path, all very nearby.

The other side of the duplex is owner-occupied by quiet, respectful, local professionals. This is a comfortable, well-cared-for home in a fantastic neighborhood.

Owner pays for city utilities (water, sewer, and garbage). Tenant pays for electric, gas, and internet. Renter’s insurance is required. Available February 1, 2026 (possibly sooner), 12-month lease. First month rent and security deposit ($2950) due at signing. No pets and no smoking.
